Algorithm Details

The optimization employs a robust three-stage strategy: 1. PGlobal (Global Search): Uses a stochastic differential evolution approach to find the global minimum region. 2. L-BFGS-B (Local Polish): Uses the Analytic Gradient to refine the solution with high precision. 3. Coordinate Descent (Fine Tuning): A final local descent step to escape narrow local minima. 4. Physical Accuracy: Rigorously accounts for Incoherent Backside Reflection in the substrate for both Transmission and Reflection (T = T_single * T_back / (1 - R_single*R_back)). The analytic gradient computes the exact derivatives of the Tauc-Lorentz-Urbach model and Transfer Matrix Method interactions (including backside effects) using the chain rule, eliminating numerical noise and providing significant performance improvements over traditional finite-difference methods.
